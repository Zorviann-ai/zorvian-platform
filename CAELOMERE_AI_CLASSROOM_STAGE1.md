# Caelomere AI Classroom — Stage 1 (rebuild)

Celeste Teacher is one identity. The Core supplies curriculum, lesson
structure, practice and progress. Whiteboard, web avatar, classroom
display and hologram are presentation surfaces.

Safeguarding is pack `caelomere-education-safeguarding-knowledge`.
It distinguishes PRIMARY LAW, STATUTORY GUIDANCE, LOCAL PROCEDURE and
ORGANISATIONAL POLICY by jurisdiction.

Celeste may detect, record, classify, explain, flag, require human
review and route. She is not a DSL, LADO, police, social services or court.

THIS STAGE DOES NOT CONTROL LIVE SCHOOL WHITEBOARDS.

THIS STAGE DOES NOT CONNECT TO HOLOGRAM HARDWARE.

THIS STAGE DOES NOT INGEST UNLICENSED COPYRIGHTED TEXTBOOKS.

THIS STAGE DOES NOT ACTIVATE EXTERNAL EXECUTION.

THIS STAGE DOES NOT DEPLOY TO PRODUCTION.

THIS STAGE DOES NOT SEND SAFEGUARDING REFERRALS OR CONTACT 999.

England child protection example:

England → Children Act 1989 → s47 → significant-harm threshold →
human review → DSL / local authority children’s social care / police.

s17 is a separate child-in-need route. Staff allegations use KCSIE Part 4
and LADO. Adult England uses Care Act 2014 s42. Scotland, Wales and
Northern Ireland use their own statutes.

Stage 4G remains frozen. This package does not import the private
production engine and does not add `/live`.
