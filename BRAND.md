## Brand idea

Caelomere is the meeting point between sky and reflection, imagination and order, intelligence and human judgement.

The identity should feel:

- celestial, intelligent and quietly powerful;
- cinematic and premium;
- creative without becoming childish;
- trustworthy without becoming cold;
- alive, conversational and accountable.

## Logo

The primary mark is the approved black-and-gold Caelomere identity: a luminous vertical core within a precise geometric intelligence structure, paired with the Caelomere wordmark.

It represents:

- intelligence, structure and purposeful connection;
- a luminous centre coordinating the wider system;
- precision without losing imagination;
- human judgement at the centre of autonomous capability.

Primary asset: `public/caelomere-official-logo.jpg`

The former three-part swirl and earlier orbit concepts are retired. They must not appear in customer-facing or internal product surfaces.

### Colour discipline

- Use Celestial Ink as the dominant field.
- Use Moonstone for readable primary content.
- Use Aurora Teal for live intelligence and interaction.
- Reserve Solar Gold for decisions, approvals and important calls to action.
- Use Nebula Violet primarily in creative and media experiences.
- Do not use every accent colour in the same component.

## Product architecture

- **Caelomere** — company and customer-facing product family.
- **Caelomere Celestial Core** — trusted intelligence and orchestration centre.
- **Caelomere CRM** — customer, project, task and activity workspace.
- **Caelomere Social Studio** — accountable social creation, approval, scheduling and analytics.
- **Caelomere TV Studio** — long-form film and television production workspace.
- **Elite AI Team** — eight senior specialist agents coordinated by the Celestial Core.

Legacy Worker, database, repository, session-cookie and environment identifiers may retain the former technical name until a controlled migration is completed. They must never be presented as the customer-facing brand.

## Experience principles

1. **Alive** — the assistant is present, responsive and optionally speaks.
2. **Simple first** — ask one clear question at a time; reveal advanced controls only when needed.
3. **Creative range** — sound, vision, humour and seriousness are first-class controls.
4. **Accountable** — preserve briefs, versions, approvals, schedules, outcomes and evidence.
5. **Human authority** — external publishing, payments, contracts and consequential actions require confirmation.
6. **Truthful status** — never report a connection, action, render or result that a real provider has not confirmed.
7. **No stock imagery** — prefer original brand assets, real product interfaces and purposeful original visual work.
8. **No generic templates** — layouts and language must feel recognisably Caelomere.

## Voice

Caelomere speaks in clear British English.

The voice is:

- calm, capable and direct;
- imaginative when creating;
- serious when risk or evidence requires it;
- humorous only when the audience and context permit;
- concise during actions and more detailed for review;
- honest about missing information, system limits and required human decisions.

Avoid hype, invented facts, false certainty and claims that external actions occurred without confirmation.

## Approved interaction language

Preferred examples:

- “What would you like to create?”
- “Would you like sound?”
- “Which channels should I prepare?”
- “Would you like me to prepare the post?”
- “Please review the facts, rights and tone.”
- “Ready for your approval.”
- “There is not enough measured evidence to recommend a posting time yet.”
